Your first 4 weeks of strength

A simple, full-body dumbbell plan using your 10–25 kg pairs and one long chair. Start light, learn the movement, and leave a few good repetitions “in the tank.”

90 kg · 183 cmEquipment: dumbbells + sturdy chair3 sessions / week · 35–45 min
Before you start: Use a chair that cannot slide or tip. Clear the floor, wear stable shoes, and stop for sharp pain, chest pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath. If you have an injury, heart condition, or have been inactive for a long time, get medical clearance first.

Weekly schedule

For example: Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Rest or walk gently on the other days.

DAY A · FULL BODY

Squat, push, pull

Goblet squat
3 sets × 8–12 reps · 10–20 kg
Hold one dumbbell at your chest; sit between your hips, then stand tall.
One-arm dumbbell row
3 × 8–12 / side · 10–20 kg
Support one hand on the chair. Pull elbow toward your hip; keep your back long.
Floor dumbbell press
3 × 8–12 · 10–15 kg each
Lie on the floor, knees bent. Lower elbows gently until triceps touch the floor.
Romanian deadlift
3 × 8–12 · 10–20 kg each
Hinge hips back with a soft knee bend; keep dumbbells close to your legs.
DAY B · FULL BODY

Hinge, press, carry

Reverse lunge
3 × 6–10 / side · bodyweight or 10 kg each
Step back softly and keep the front knee tracking over your middle toes.
Standing overhead press
3 × 6–10 · 10 kg each
Brace your ribs down; press without leaning back. Use one dumbbell if needed.
Chair-supported split squat
2 × 8–10 / side · bodyweight or 10 kg
Use the chair only for balance. Lower under control, pushing through the front foot.
Farmer carry
3 rounds × 30–45 sec · 15–25 kg each
Walk slowly and tall. Stop before your grip or posture breaks.
DAY C · FULL BODY

Strength and control

Dumbbell deadlift
3 × 8–12 · 15–25 kg each
Brace before lifting; stand by driving the floor away. Do not round your back.
Incline push-up on chair
3 × 6–12 · bodyweight
Hands on a stable chair, body straight. Use a wall or floor if the chair moves.
Seated curl
2 × 10–15 · 10 kg each
Sit tall, keep elbows still, and lower slowly. Choose 5–8 kg if 10 kg is too heavy.
Suitcase carry
3 × 30 sec / side · 15–25 kg
Hold one dumbbell; resist leaning. Switch sides each round.

Every session

Quality beats load. Rest 60–120 seconds between sets.

5-minute warm-up

  • 1 minute easy marching
  • 10 bodyweight squats
  • 10 hip hinges
  • 8 wall or chair push-ups
  • Arm circles and ankle rolls

How to progress

  • Weeks 1–2: 2 sets if you are very sore; otherwise use the listed sets.
  • When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 1–2 reps or move up one dumbbell size.
  • Keep 2–3 reps in reserve; never force a failed rep.
