# Clause Library: ตัวอย่างข้อความที่ควรมี

## 12.1 Scope Protection

```text
Any work not expressly stated in this Agreement or the attached Scope of Work shall be considered outside the scope. Additional work shall require written approval by both parties, including additional fees and adjusted timeline.
```

## 12.2 Payment Protection

```text
The Contractor may suspend work or withhold final deliverables, source code, production files, credentials, or release package if payment is delayed or not completed according to the agreed payment schedule.
```

## 12.3 IP Transfer After Full Payment

```text
All final deliverables specifically created for the Client shall be transferred to the Client only after the Contractor has received full payment. Until full payment is received, all rights in the work remain with the Contractor.
```

## 12.4 Acceptance

```text
The Client shall review each deliverable and provide written feedback within five (5) business days. If no feedback is provided within this period, the deliverable shall be deemed accepted.
```

## 12.5 Change Request

```text
Any change to the scope, features, platform, design, timeline, or deliverables must be agreed in writing before the Contractor starts the additional work.
```

## 12.6 Client Delay

```text
Any delay caused by the Client’s late feedback, missing assets, delayed approval, or delayed payment shall extend the delivery timeline accordingly.
```

## 12.7 Termination Payment

```text
If the Client terminates the Agreement after work has started, the Client shall pay for all completed work, work in progress, and committed time up to the termination date.
```

## 12.8 Reusable Code

```text
The Contractor may continue to use general knowledge, skills, tools, reusable code, libraries, frameworks, and techniques that are not confidential information of the Client.
```
